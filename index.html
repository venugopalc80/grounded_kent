01 / THE GROUNDING

NOT JUST A COFFEE STOP

Come for the coffee.
Stay for the feeling.

Grounded is an independent café at the heart of Weldon Heart. A place for good coffee, thoughtful food, premium matcha and the simple pleasure of being around people.

Run by a yoga teacher with community at its core, Grounded brings together coffee, wellness and connection in one welcoming space.
